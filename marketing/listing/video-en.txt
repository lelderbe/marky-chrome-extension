Title: Marky — Save Chrome Bookmarks to the Right Folder

Open Marky, type a folder name, and press Enter. This short demo shows saving a page, filtering bookmark folders, and creating a new folder when there are no matches.

Marky works with your existing Chrome bookmarks. No separate account or import. No Marky analytics.

Install: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ekklkmikngajdgklihnnhcjmbjoefaem?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=keyboard-bookmarking
Source and support: https://github.com/lelderbe/marky-chrome-extension

Suggested shortcut: Command+B on macOS or Ctrl+B on Windows/Linux. Assign it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts if needed.

Edited demo using the Marky interface with sample bookmark folders. The browser backdrop is illustrative.
